WHO WE ARE

A Medium Voltage Switchgear manufacturer built on three decades of expertise

SRIWIN ELECTRIC is an established Medium Voltage Switchgear Manufacturing company, based in Salem, Tamil Nadu, India. The promoter has more than 30 years of experience in the design, development & testing of a wide variety of MV Switchgear, with a strong focus on indigenous R&D.

The unit has state-of-the-art infrastructure for manufacturing & testing, and is ISO 9001:2015 certified. All products comply with relevant IS/IEC/IEEE/ANSI Standards and are type-tested as per relevant standards.

The organisation is enriched with a team of well-qualified engineers & technicians, who bring decades of experience in the Medium Voltage Switchgear industry as well as on the application side.
